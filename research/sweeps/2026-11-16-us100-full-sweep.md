**The main finding.** Moving TP1 to 1 R lifts every model to 45–49% wins, and the mean R lands at about −0.03: what a coin flip pays after costs. The win rate is a function of the target distance; it is not evidence of an edge. The entries, as they stand, do not predict direction better than chance. Getting to "50% with positive R" needs entries that win more than 50% at 1 R, i.e. a real edge at the entry, not a nearer target.

The one place a filter adds expectancy: the 2022 model with risk ≥ 0.05 ADR (+0.20 R, z 2.0, in-sample, one asset).
