# Worked example: US100 at the New York open, 17 Nov 2026

Read with `2026-11-17.md` (the running log) and the hunting playbook. Three things happened on one chart in four hours; each was a lesson Sharif gave live.

## 1. The sell I did not see (06:40–07:00)
- **Area:** Monday's 4h bearish gap, 27,316.6–27,400.6, inside the old 5 Nov daily gap.
- **Liquidity against it:** Monday evening's equal highs, 27,354.0 / 27,355.8, inside the gap.
- **Eaten:** the 06:00 rally (83% hourly body) went into the gap and took them at 06:40 (27,382.4).
- **Block:** the 06:40 5m candle (27,344.5–27,369.0), engulfed at 06:45 (95% body).
- **Then:** 27,382 → 27,114.5 by 09:52.
- **Why I missed it:** I filed the 06:00 rally under "a strong reversal up" and never asked what it had eaten. US500's same rally stopped under its equal highs (created, not eaten), which is how Sharif made me see the difference.

## 2. Eaten or created, and both can come first
- US100 ate its highs and sold. US500 left its highs (7,435–7,437, at the edge of its own 4h gap) and sold anyway.
- So the sweep is never a condition: hold both cases and say which one the market is doing.

## 3. The sell I saw late (09:40–09:52), paper idea NY-1
- **Area:** the opening drop's own 5m gap, 27,212.5–27,254.0 ("the 5min FVG was a nice touch").
- **Candle closes:** 09:40, a wick through the gap to 27,267.2, close 27,144.6.
- **1-minute:** structure broke down 09:43–09:44 and left a gap, 27,188.6–27,227.3.
- **Entry:** the returns into that gap, 09:45–09:49 (four of them). I placed the order at 09:52.
- **Result:** the equal lows (my TP1, the range low) went at 09:52, 10 points before my entry was touched: a missed trade.
- **Rule taken:** in a fast market the order goes on at the first return; the write-up comes after.

## 4. The conditions I did not name
- From 09:30 to 10:00 US100 travelled 821 points to end 28 from where it started, while US30 rallied the other way.
- I had written "pace: aggressive". Sharif: "the market is still very consolidative... it's very choppy"; knowing whether the market is moving cleanly and fast enough to even consider a trade is its own skill.
- So NY-1 was a clean read taken in poor conditions, and should have been labelled that way.

## 5. Judged on their own timeframes (the 10:00 closes)
- The 4h gap held: one 4h wick in (27,382.4), no 4h body inside.
- I called the 06:00 hour's gap "dead" at 09:37 from 5m prices; its hour had not closed. It closed with the body inside the gap but above its middle: weaker, not broken.
