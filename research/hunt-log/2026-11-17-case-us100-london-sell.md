## The context, top-down
- **Long time (monthly):** two rising gaps from the April–May rally. May's (27,490–28,234) had been traded through; price was entering April's (25,208–27,408) from the top. A valid buy area; whether it would break a bit lower first or be bought at once was open. July's low 27,063 just below.
- **Short time (weekly / daily):** lower highs since 31,365. Monday closed down through the 9 Nov daily gap (27,319–27,420), which then resisted.
- **With or against:** every sell here was with the short term and against the long term. It played out as "break a bit lower first".
- **4h:** the old 5 Nov gap (27,319–27,495) was support on 10 Nov, broken on 16 Nov (close 27,317), then respected from below three candles running: wicks in to 27,356 and 27,414, no body inside. Under it a **dim** order block (the 18:00 up candle, more wick than body).
- **The three indices:** at 01:00 US500 and US30 made lower lows and US100 kept its 18:00 low (SMT = divergence). US500 never retested its old gap; US100 did, which made US100 the interesting chart.
- **Pace:** slow all session, one aggressive up candle at 02:54–02:55, then one aggressive down candle at 04:00. Stronger conditions are wanted; this is why New York is favoured.

## The entry (way in 3), in the order it has to be read
1. **A candle closes that makes the area look important.** After five red 5m candles, a small bullish candle closed at 03:50 (27,170.3 → 27,178.8). By itself it says little.
2. **Look back.** The rally to 27,288 had launched at 01:30–01:40; the 01:40 up candle left a 5m gap at 27,177.6–27,182.6. At 03:40 price had closed below that gap, so it had flipped. The bullish candle was the retest from below: close inside the gap, wicks to 27,184.6 and 27,184.7, **no body back above it**. Respected.
3. **Drop to the 1-minute.**
4. **Break of structure on the 1m.** The last small low inside the pause was 27,160.6 (03:49). The 03:55 candle fell from 27,179.0 and the 03:56 candle closed at 27,157.0, under it.
5. **The gap that break left** is the entry: 27,167.2–27,170.4 (03:54 low to 03:56 high). Price came back to it at 03:58 (27,169.1), 03:59 (27,174.7) and 04:00 (27,173.3).
6. **Stop** above the high made in the zone, 27,184.7 (about 15 points). **Targets:** the overnight low 27,098.5 (about 5 R), July's low 27,063 (about 7 R); price reached 27,002 by 04:20.

Why the retrace was shallow: price was already 25 points from the liquidity it was reaching for (the range low 27,143, then the overnight low). Close to the liquidity, do not expect a deep retrace.

## Liquidity along the way: swept or created
- Swept: small equal highs 27,250 (02:55, inside the 4h block); equal lows 27,200 (03:12), 27,191 (03:32), 27,168 (03:49); the range low 27,143 (03:57); the overnight low and July's low (04:00).
- Created and left behind: equal highs at 27,246–27,249, on the underside of the 5m gap 27,248.9–27,253.7, below the 5m block. Price never went back for them.

## What I got wrong, so I do not repeat it
- I called the bias "down" from the weekly and daily and did not open the monthly.
- I treated the 5m block as the first trade; the move had begun at the higher-timeframe gap.
- I waited for a deep retrace into a block 90 points behind price while price was already next to its target.
- At 03:50 I studied the candles in front of me (an order block, a sweep, a breaker) and did not look back to where the last move had started and the gap it left. It took four hints.
- I drew a gap before its third candle had closed, and I wrote readings into my notes before he had confirmed them.

## Rules that came out of it
- A missed trade is missed once the trading range breaks without the entry being hit; no chasing. Being on the wrong side is part of the process.
- A re-entry is a separate trade inside the same move, for the small piece that is left.
- The most important key: where is price right now.

## Sharif's review, the same evening (answers in `2026-11-17-ny1-discussion.md`, point 11)
- **The 03:07 sell was a good trade and an accepted miss:** no premium, but the middle was enough.
- **The tip I missed:** the small equal highs at 27,246–27,249 meant price was creating liquidity, not eating it, so that area would not be swept soon.
- **The order stays on** until a major pool is hit and the retrace clearly will not come; a closed candle below the 01:40 gap (27,177–27,182) would have ended it.
- **The re-entry, in his order:** equal highs created → the equal low 27,191.27 broken (structure) → a 5m candle closes with its body inside the 01:40 gap → 1m displacement and break → sell limit in the 1m gap (27,167.2–27,169.5), stop above the 5m gap (27,182.3). At 03:57 price tipped the higher low (27,143.14 against 27,143.31) without taking it, then came back and filled the order at 03:58.
- **Not a chase:** the higher low had been the larger goal since 03:20 and was still ahead; the 00:25 high was unbroken.
- **The money:** 80% at the higher low (the real range target; passed 04:01, about 1.6 R on a 15.7-point risk), 20% as the runner (overnight low 04:03, 4.5 R; July low 04:04, 6.7 R; the low 27,001.8, 10.6 R).
- **Corrected by Sharif:** two "S" trades after the missed one, not one. First the 03:30 one: equal highs created above (27,246–27,249), the 03:30 1m candle displaces down through structure and leaves a gap 27,206.2–27,223.7, price returns into it at 03:33 and 03:35, then falls. His rule: when price is creating liquidity, look for displacement on the opposite side, its break of structure, and the return to the array it leaves. Second, the 03:58 one above.
