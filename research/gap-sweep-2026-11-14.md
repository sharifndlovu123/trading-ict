Selection period only: 2022-01-03 → 2024-06-28, every model's newest run (`store.latest`), both cost bases.
Tool: `uv run python -m ict_engine.eval.gaps --cost {cfd,futures}`. Full tables: `reports/gaps-{cfd,futures}.md`.

## 2. "Stopped, then TP1 later" is volatility, not stop hunting
Control (`research/scripts/stop_after_control.py`): after a stop-out, measured from the stop price, how often TP1
trades before 16:00 vs a level the **same distance on the other side**. They are equal for every model on both
bases, e.g. PD array return futures 0.50 vs 0.51, Cameron 0.56 vs 0.57. Price data shows no stop-hunt signature,
so wider or shifted stops can't recover this. The earlier `cf_wide_stop` and direction diagnosis said the same.

## 3. "Missed" is the real gap, and it belongs to the 2022 model
Counterfactual (`research/scripts/market_entry_counterfactual.py`): every setup that placed an order, entered at
**market** at the open of the bar after the decision. Same stop, same TP1, R after costs on the new risk. First
order per model/asset/day/window only (as live), standard errors clustered by day.

| Model | Limit (actual) | Market, CFD (z_cl) | Market, futures (z_cl) | Futures 2022-01→23-03 / 23-04→24-06 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2022 model** | +0.036 / −0.058 | +0.049 (1.7) | **+0.095 (5.2)** | +0.085 (3.4) / +0.108 (3.9) |
| Cameron | +0.004 / −0.099 | +0.007 (0.2) | −0.088 (−3.5) | −0.102 / −0.070 |
| MMxM | −0.068 / −0.064 | −0.040 (−1.0) | −0.027 (−0.9) | −0.014 / −0.045 |
| PD array return | −0.060 / −0.027 | −0.052 (−1.4) | −0.035 (−1.4) | −0.061 / +0.001 |
| Silver Bullet | −0.045 / +0.001 | −0.052 (−1.5) | −0.026 (−0.9) | −0.073 / +0.027 |

**Reading**
- Only the 2022 model improves, and only it has a large "missed" share. Its FVG limit fills mostly when price
  comes back (the losers), while the winners leave without it. This is **adverse selection of passive orders**
  (Harris ch.4 and ch.13–14, `references/orderflow.md` §11): a limit order's fills are selected against it.
- The futures result is 1 of 10 counterfactual tests (5 models × 2 bases). z 5.2 clears any multiple-test
  allowance, and both halves agree.
- Caveat: this is a script, not the engine's simulator (fill on the next bar's open, no slippage beyond the cost
  model). **Next:** build `entry_mode="market"` into the simulator and the 2022 model, run it through the pipeline
  on 2022–24 with the in-period hold-out, then pre-register it as a forward-test candidate (`forward-v2`, own clock).
  The burned 2024-07+ period may be shown as a sanity check only, never as confirmation.

## 4. Where order flow comes in (hypotheses for when CME TBBO data exists)
| Gap | Order-flow question |
|---|---|
| missed (2022 model) | Does one-sided aggressor flow at the trigger predict that the limit won't fill? Then go market only when flow is one-sided, and keep the limit otherwise |
| fast loss | Is a trigger FVG printed against the aggressor delta / OFI a trap? |
| stop → TP1 | Price can't separate hunted stops from noise (§2). Does absorption (high volume, no follow-through) at the stop mark the ones that reverse? |
| near miss / slow loss | Does delta exhaustion before TP1 predict the turn? (Expect small: exits change shape, not mean) |
