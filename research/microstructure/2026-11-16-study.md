# Microstructure components tested on our trades — 2026-11-16

**Result: no component separates wins from losses.** 26 components, 52 tests (mean R and win rate for each), false-discovery correction at q 0.10: **0 pass**. The largest |z| of the 52 is 2.5, about what the best of 52 tests of pure noise gives. The only microstructure fact that helps is the one we already have (market activity); the volatility and volume measures are other ways of writing it. Nothing here becomes a gate, a stop rule or a target rule.

- Components, formulas, sources: `research/microstructure/components.md`
- Code: `research/scripts/micro_features.py` (`build` → `reports/micro-features-2026-11-16.parquet`, one row per trade; `study` → `results-2026-11-16.md` / `.csv` in this folder with every column)
- Tests: `tests/test_micro_features.py` (50 tests: known answers on hand-built bars, and a no-look-ahead proof for each feature)

| Multiple testing | Benjamini–Hochberg at q 0.10 over all 52 p-values. With 52 tests, about 2.5 are expected beyond \|z\| 2 by chance; we saw 2 |

Feature windows (60 minutes for most, 120 for the variance ratio and the long flow window) were fixed before looking at outcomes and not tuned. Section 5 lists the few extra looks made afterwards.

### Reading the four that came closest
- **Spread vs normal** (z 1.7 on R, 1.8 on win; right sign in 6 and 7 of 7 datasets; independent of activity). It looks consistent, but the feature hardly moves: 90 % of trades sit between 0.96 and 1.04, because the CFD spread is the broker's schedule and not a market being stressed. An effect of 3 points from a 4 % wobble in a scheduled number has no mechanism behind it, and z 1.7 is ordinary for the best of 15. By model: MMxM +8.0 points (z 2.1), silver bullet +3.4 (z 1.3), 2022 model 0.0. Verdict: no value. A real spread test needs futures quotes.
- **Tick-rule flow with the trade** (win +3.5 points, z 2.1, 7 of 7 datasets; R +0.02). The win rate rises and R does not. The top third has nearer targets (median TP1 1.85 R vs 2.00 R), and on the equal-target counterfactual (TP1 at 1 R) the thirds win 49.9 / 47.7 / 50.2 %. So it is target distance again, and says nothing about direction. No value.
- **Normal range for the clock time** (win −4.2 points, z −2.5, 6 of 7; R −0.01). This is the fill hour (ρ +0.68 with the hour): high-volatility hours have a lower win rate and the same R. It repeats "win rate moves with time and target, R does not". No value beyond the window rules we already have.
- **Realized / high-low volatility** have the same sign as activity (6 of 7 datasets for `rv_rel`) and are weaker than it. ρ 0.79 and 0.78 with `act_rel`, and nothing is left once activity is known. Duplicates.

`kyle_t` on bars is not a real measurement: the tick rule takes the sign from the same price change the regression explains, so t is between 2 and 15 on every trade.

### The open idea: flow over the whole approach
- **Cumulative delta with the trade over 30 / 60 / 120 minutes: no support.** Over 60 and 120 minutes there is nothing (z −0.1, −0.8; the 120-minute sign flips between quarters). Over 30 minutes the sign is **against** the idea: trades entered after the flow ran *with* them earned −0.16 R, those entered after flow *against* them +0.27 R (z −1.7, same sign in both quarters: Q1 +0.22 vs −0.21, Q2 +0.31 vs −0.12). The tercile cuts are small (I = −0.014 / +0.025).
- That is the same direction as the failed H2 on the full year (flow with the trade at the trigger earned less, z −0.5). Two looks at overlapping trades pointing the same way is a lead, not a finding: z 1.7 on 82 vs 82 trades, one of 52 tests.
- **Delta divergence into the entry: untestable at this size.** Price made a new extreme in the last 15 minutes on 72 of 246 trades; delta diverged on 20 of them. Those 20: 35 % win, −0.05 R, against 38.5 % and +0.14 R when the flow confirmed the new extreme. The sign flips between quarters (Q1 7 trades, Q2 13).
- **Absorption on the approach: 26 trades, worse if anything** (27 % win, −0.18 R). It agrees with H3 (absorption at stop-outs: nothing).
- **VPIN** flips between quarters (Q1: bottom third 11 % win, top 41 %; Q2: 47 % vs 40 %). With the exchange's own aggressor side it sits between 0.03 and 0.06 on NQ, far below the bulk-classified values in the papers, and shows what Andersen–Bondarenko say: it carries no information of its own here.
- **Real traded volume vs normal** is activity again (ρ 0.70 with `act_rel`): the quiet third 27 % win, −0.33 R; right sign in both quarters. It confirms the activity finding on a second instrument's volume. It adds nothing new.

## 5. Honest accounting
- **Tests:** 52 in the corrected family (26 components × R and win). 0 pass at q 0.10. The reference row (`act_rel`) is not counted.
- **Extra looks after the tables** (not in the family; they would only raise the bar): per-model and per-dataset splits of the three near-misses; the 1 R counterfactual for `tick_imb`; "quiet" thresholds (< 0.6) on `rv_rel`, `pk_rel`, `intensity_rel`; quarter splits of five flow features.
  - One oddity from those: Dukascopy volume below 0.6 of normal went with *better* trades (n 400, 39.2 % win, +0.18 R, z 2.4), the opposite of activity, with only 24 % overlap with activity-quiet. Dukascopy "volume" is the quote feed's size number, not contracts traded, and the result came from an unplanned threshold. Treated as noise.
- **Activity itself is modest in this framing:** by thirds, +0.108 R (z 2.0; clustered 1.7), win rate flat. The known result is at the quiet threshold: `act_rel` < 0.6 → 30.2 % win, −0.166 R (n 377) against 33.5 %, +0.016 R (z −2.4) on this trade set.
- **Independence:** z values assume independent trades. On the bar set the day-clustered z is about 10–20 % smaller; none of the conclusions depends on which is used.
- **Fill time vs decision time:** features are at the fill minute. For a limit order that is usable live (the order can be pulled the minute before), but it is later than `decided_at`. Nothing passed, so the choice does not matter here.
- **Power:** on the bar set (≈ 1,550 per third, R sd ≈ 1.5) a difference of about 0.15 R would show at z 2.8. Effects that size are ruled out for every bar component. On the flow set (82 per third) only differences above ≈ 0.65 R would; smaller flow effects can't be ruled in or out with one half-year.
- **Side finding (not fixed, outside this task's files):** `research/scripts/time_activity.py` steps back with `t − pd.Timedelta(days=k)`, which is 24 h of absolute time. For about four weeks after each clock change the "same clock time" baseline is partly one hour off. `micro_features.seasonal` uses `pd.DateOffset(days=k)` (wall clock) and has a test for it. `ict_engine/concepts/activity.py` line 33 has the same step (`at - pd.Timedelta(days=k)`), so the `min_activity` gate is affected too. Small, but worth fixing through the change gate before the 2021 activity run.

## 6. What this means for the engine
- **No new gate, stop rule or target rule.** No component is positive, so there is nothing to confirm.
- **Keep the activity gate as the single "is the market moving" measure.** Realized volatility, high-low volatility and real volume say the same thing with less separation. Don't add them beside it: correlated gates only multiply the trials.
- **Leads, in order of how cheap they are to settle**
  1. *Flow against the trade in the last 30 minutes* (enter after the pullback's flow, not after flow already with the trade). Twice now the sign has pointed this way (H2 on 2024, `imb30` here). How it would enter: a tag on every backtest row first; a gate only after confirmation. How to confirm: one pre-registered test on US100 2024-07 → 2024-12 with the NQ flow already on disk — same feature, same tercile cuts (−0.014 / +0.025), pass at one-sided z ≥ 2.4 with day-clustered errors. That period is a reserved hold-out, so it needs Sharif's go-ahead. Expect it to fail: the lead is z 1.7.
  2. *Real-volume activity on NQ* as a cross-check of the activity gate, inside the already planned 2021 confirmation run only if CME bars for 2021 are bought; otherwise skip. It is a duplicate, not a new rule.
  3. *A real spread / adverse-selection test* needs futures quotes (below). Parked.
- **The books' own warning held** (orderflow.md §9–10): order-flow information lives at seconds, our trades last minutes to hours, and "expect tiny fit". The entries still carry no forecast of direction; microstructure state at the fill does not supply one.
