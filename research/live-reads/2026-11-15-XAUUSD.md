### Built from this (2026-11-16)
- `ict_engine/concepts/htf_draw.py` — `bos_draw(bars, tf, as_of)`. It runs the chain: BOS (a close through the latest
  confirmed swing; a wick is not a break), then relative equal lows/highs beyond the broken level (candle lows, not
  only 3-bar swings; nothing traded through between them), then the nearest remaining FVG beyond them. A later close
  through an EQ member is **delivery, not a new BOS** (the chain is kept, and the EQ is marked taken). On gold it
  gives exactly his chain from July on.
- `ob_context._place`: premium/discount of the **dealing range the OB's move retraced into**: the last swing high
  above the move's extreme → the lowest low since. It falls back to the look-back window at a new high, and the
  window's reading is kept as `lookback`. Gold 9 Nov: premium 0.71 (the window had said 0.44).
- `ob_context._draw(..., htf=[(monthly, "1M"), (weekly, "1w")])`: the draw comes from the highest timeframe that has a
  remaining gap. Gold daily OB → the **1M FVG 3,453.6–3,819.1**. The gap's state is judged on its own timeframe's
  closes; edge liquidity uses untaken levels only.
- Engine read: a "Monthly" statement comes first (BOS → EQ → draw, with chart lines). Daily OBs are added. The play
  gets "the higher-TF draw (monthly) … near edge = the target". Light theme by default (toggle). OB boxes show only on
  their own timeframe.
- Tests: `tests/test_htf_draw.py` — hand-built (BOS, EQ, delivery ≠ new BOS, wick ≠ break, no look-ahead, dealing-range
  place), plus his gold chain and daily OB on real data (before the fill; no OB before the engulfing close).
