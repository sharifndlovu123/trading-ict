The 09:30 spike was the Judas into the order. The next two candles fell 38 points; TP1 at 09:32, then the London low, the Asia low and TP2 by 10:25, and a weekly swing low by the evening (20 R from the turn). The pre-open drift was noise in front of the real move.

## Would a better cancel rule have caught it?
No. Run 0.078 ADR, no pool taken. A time limit would have hurt: 21 minutes is the wait for the 09:30 open, which is the whole point of a pre-open order. A cancel rule should not count pre-open drift before a known volatility event.
