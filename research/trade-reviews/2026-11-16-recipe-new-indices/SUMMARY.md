# 30 recipe trades on the unseen indices reviewed (Russell 2000, DAX, FTSE)

The run: `research/preregistered-recipe-new-indices.md` (US2000 +0.06 R, DE40 +0.11, UK100 −0.20; the volatile-or-news split did not repeat). Two reviewers, 15 trades each. Leads, not evidence.

## What they found
- **No look-ahead** (one batch re-derived all 15 trigger gaps from 15m candles closed at the decision time).
- **Losers (15):** stop on a session or swing extreme taken and TP1 traded later (6); a long in premium into an opposing 4h/1d bearish FVG (3); against the day's wave or the daily (3); a quiet day with a target larger than the range left (2); late for the day (1). The stop sat exactly on a pool in 12 of 15 trades of one batch; the entry was in the wrong half of the range in 11 of 15.
- **Winners (15):** the process wins were shorts with 4h and 1h bear, a rally into a bearish FVG to its 50% and a real 15m displacement. About half of the rest are thin: 1 R scalps of 4–6 points on the Russell decided within minutes, stops of 0.04–0.06 ADR at the open, two time exits.
- **Would Sharif take it?** yes 3, maybe 13, no 14. The "no" group: 2 thin wins and most of the losses.

## The DAX and the FTSE: the recipe does not fit them
- Their range is made 03:00–08:00 New York; the New York morning window catches the last two hours.
- 8 of 17 DE40 / UK100 trades were decided at or after the 11:30 cash close; the 16:00 flat holds 4.5 hours past it.
- No European calendar (ECB, UK data); "previous day" levels can come from a holiday session.
- Trigger gaps below one futures tick on the FTSE (0.40 points) and the DAX (0.56).
A recipe for them would need their own window (London morning) and calendar. Not done.
