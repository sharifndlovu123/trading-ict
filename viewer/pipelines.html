Engine pipelines

Which skill runs when, for each kind of job. Goal: ≥ 50% win rate (TP1 before the stop) with positive R, day-only holds, proven on unseen data.

A · Backtest

After any backtest, sweep or simulation.
1 RunIn-sample only, futures cost. Keep the previous result to compare.
→
2 ScoreTrades, win % (range), mean R, z, capped R, change vs before.
→
3 Review facts, all tradesSide vs read, range half, sweep before, stop vs pools, pools before TP1.
→
4 Pattern finderWhich facts separate wins from losses. On R and on win rate.
→
5 Written reviews, at least 30 trades15 winners + 15 losers, after every backtest or major change. The reason behind the numbers.
→
6 Tweak labReplay every trade with tweaked stops, targets, entries. How many trades does each tweak fix or break?
→
7 Array auditIf problems cluster on one array, or a trade can't be explained.
→
8 Findings → BEach recommendation and tweak goes through the change gate.
Every analysis is also reported by layer: time and activity (entry hour; quiet / normal / busy) · price (where price really turned, and which known level sat there) · microstructure (order flow, where futures trade data exists).

Decision rules: scores → next step

#When the scores showNext step
R1Win ≥ 50%, mean R > 0, z ≥ 2, holds capped and in both halvesCandidate → pipeline E. No more tuning in-sample.
R2Mean R < 0 with z ≤ −2, or negative in most cutsConsistent loser: stop tuning. One pattern-finder pass for a "do less" filter; if none holds, park it.
R3No edge (|z| < 2)Review facts + pattern finder + written reviews. The checklist step that fails most becomes the next option to test.
R4"Do less" on one array kind/TF (n ≥ 30), or ≥ 3 of 12 reviews fail the array stepArray audit before concluding anything about that array.
R5One trade the review can't explain, or the array disagrees with the chartArray audit for that moment; wrong array → bug fix through B.
R6Any recommendation to change codePipeline B. Never straight from a finding.
R7A code change landedRe-run the affected backtest and compare.
R8Win rate and R disagreeCheck target and stop distance first; test smaller targets / structure stops as options.
R10A tweak fixes many more trades than it breaks and lifts R on most datasetsOption behind a flag → real backtest → confirmation. Never a default straight from the screen.
R11A tweak changes win rate but not RNot an improvement. Look at which setups and when, not stop/target arithmetic.
R9Day-only R very different from held RReport both. A rule that only works overnight is not a candidate.
