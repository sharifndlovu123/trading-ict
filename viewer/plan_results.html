Each trade: 50 % off at TP1, stop to entry, the rest to the final target or the horizon close. Same-minute stop and target count as the stop.

Reading it

  • Filling on the first touch loses clearly (−0.15 R a trade, z −4.3). That part is solid.
  • The confirmation "profits" are a few outsized trades, not an edge. The jump in June 2022 is a handful of US30/US500 trades of +19 to +27 R (tiny confirmation stops, price ran to the final target). Take the 3 best out and confirm is −73 R, confirm + flip −15 R; cap every trade at 10 R and both are negative. The median trade in every mode is −1 R.
  • The "support" entry is too early: 403 of 460 take the full −1 R. The first lower-timeframe FVG after the touch comes on the first wiggle, and the stop sits just past it.
  • The flip leg holds up best: 34 % win, still +32 R with each trade capped at 10 R, about +2 R without its 3 best trades. Worth working on, not proven.
  • Plans from fast moves and from order-block zones lose in every mode; the daily range does better than the 4H.
  • Consecutive days often re-plan the same zone, so some trades repeat (the 1 and 2 Jun 2022 US30 trades close together). The z-scores overstate how sure we are. All of this is in-sample: it tells us what to fix, not what works.
