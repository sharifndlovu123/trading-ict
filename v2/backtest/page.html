How to read this. A call is one sentence the tools made at a past moment, such as "price might draw to the buyside". It has no entry, stop or target. The nearer of two levels usually goes first whatever a tool says, so every table is split by which was nearer. A share is only of the calls that resolved one way or the other, and the count is beside it. These are observations about the tools, not rules.
